Instructor dashboard (optional)

If your instructor uses a NO CAP dashboard, connect here so they can see incidents as they happen. Nothing is sent until you connect and agree below.

While connected, the exam form and rules come from your instructor, and your incident records (times, categories and website domains) are sent to . No page addresses, answers or form content are sent.

Chrome will ask you to allow NO CAP to contact that address. Plain http:// works only for localhost and school network addresses.

Detection

Tries to close newly opened tabs and return the student to the exam tab. It is best effort only: a student can still bypass it (for example by using another browser), so it does not make cheating impossible.

Chrome losing focus for less than this is ignored, which avoids false alarms from accidental clicks and system pop-ups. Set 0 to record every loss.

The session is flagged for review when this many incidents are recorded. Set 0 to turn the flag off. A flag is not a finding of misconduct.

Prohibited websites

Checked against the domain of the tab the student is viewing. Subdomains match too. Only the domain is stored, never the full address.

    Allowed exceptions

    Sites other than the exam form that are allowed during the exam. Google sign-in is allowed by default so forms that require login keep working. Other Google sites, including Docs, are not allowed just because they share the Google domain.

      Local data

      Session logs live in this browser profile only. Nothing is uploaded. Logs are kept until you delete them here.

      Open session log

      What NO CAP does not do

      It cannot see other applications, other devices, other browsers or other Chrome profiles, and it does not take screenshots, log keystrokes or read the form. Its records are supporting evidence, not tamper-proof proof. For stronger control, use a separately managed examination environment.
